## Decisions requiring boss/product confirmation

### P01 — Authoritative mode-state contract

Choose the eventual backend/app mode field and how an active trainer/program relationship establishes ownership. The approved principle is fixed; no new resolver schema or casual-text fallback has been invented.

### P02 — Owned marketplace display/commercial rules

Confirm how an already-owned marketplace program is displayed relative to the RYX subscription tier. The candidate decides RYX-generated coaching depth, preserves current backend catalog/library/stub contracts and changes no owned-content unlock policy or pricing.

### P03 — Billing failure and ledger edge policy

Confirm failed/cancelled turns, concurrent balance checks, retries, refunds and ledger/idempotency semantics beyond the approved final-action cost/verify/charge-once/persist sequence. No new fee/refund or transaction schema is invented.

### P04 — Future durable preference/client-note memory

Confirm future storage scope, retention and permission design if that feature is later implemented. Current capabilities are described truthfully; this is not a current Pro promise or a newly approved feature.

These are four additional future choices outside the approved H01–H12 scope, not unresolved contradictions in the canonical instructions. In particular, the matrix does decide RYX-generated Free/Pro content; it does not create a new owned-marketplace-content commercial policy, mode schema, refund policy or durable preference feature.
